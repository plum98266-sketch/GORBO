Questions

Is this an official app?

No. POB Map is an independent, fan-focused tool. It isn't affiliated with any agency, artist, or store.

Will you host photocard images?

No official images are copied. Cards are described by name and set, and your binder uses photos you take yourself.

How much will it cost?

The POB map and basic binder will be free. We'll ask the waitlist before deciding on any paid extras.
